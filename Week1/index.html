Classic Vanilla Cake

Ingredients

  • 2 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
  • 2 cups sugar
  • 1 cup unsalted butter (softened)
  • 4 large eggs
  • 1 cup milk
  • 1 tablespoon baking powder
  • 2 teaspoons vanilla extract
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt

Instructions

  1. Preheat your oven to 350°F (180°C). Grease and flour two 9-inch cake pans.
  2. In a bowl, mix flour, baking powder, and salt.
  3. In another bowl, beat the butter and sugar together until light and fluffy.
  4. Add the eggs one at a time, mixing well after each addition.
  5. Stir in the vanilla extract.
  6. Gradually add the dry mixture alternating with the milk. Mix until smooth.
  7. Pour the batter evenly into the cake pans.
  8. Bake for 25–30 minutes or until a toothpick comes out clean.
  9. Allow the cakes to cool before frosting or serving.
